**Budget:** 40% of Meta spend

**Audiences:**

| Ad Format | Creative Hook | Est. CPL |
|---|---|---|

| Founder Reel | "My mom waited 20 years to travel. We built TripNaari." | ₹250–₹500 |

> **Lead Form Best Practice:** Use exactly 3 qualifying questions: (1) Which destination? (2) When are you planning? (3) Solo or group? This filters tire-kickers and keeps CPL low while improving lead quality.

**KPIs:** CTR > 2.5% | CPL < ₹350 | Lead form fill rate > 35%
